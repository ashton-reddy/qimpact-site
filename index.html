HostAshton Reddy
Why this show

Asking the questions a curious outsider would

Ashton Reddy is a junior at Phillips Exeter Academy and founder of the Exeter Quantum Computing Club. He came to quantum through error-correction research. Most quantum conversations are about qubit counts and funding rounds. This one starts with the problem: a patient's scan, a jammed GPS signal, a bank's security, a farm's fertilizer bill.

  • SchoolPhillips Exeter Academy, class of 2028
  • FounderExeter Quantum Computing Club
  • ResearchQuantum error correction and quantum sensing
  • First paperPredicting failures in a noisy error-correcting code with a neural network
  • Current workAdaptive Error-Corrected Quantum Sensing Under Nonstationary Noise: An Application to Human Biomagnetic Signals
